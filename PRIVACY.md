# RuleBridge — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 10 November 2026. Developer: Buahrasa Labs. Contact: buahrasa01@gmail.com*

## What RuleBridge does

RuleBridge is an Atlassian Forge app for Confluence. It checks Confluence pages against consistency rules and shows findings, with optional one-click, human-confirmed fixes, inside Confluence.

## Data the app accesses

The app accesses Confluence pages and content properties, searches content, and can apply labels, through Atlassian's APIs, acting as the user who is viewing or running it (asUser). This processing happens entirely inside Atlassian's Forge platform.

## Data storage and transfer

- The developer operates no servers for this app and receives no data from it.
- The app uses no external storage, no third-party services, no analytics, and no AI/LLM services. No data leaves Atlassian's platform.
- Scan results are written as content properties and labels inside your own Confluence site only. The app does not store personal data in storage controlled by the developer.

## Your choices

Uninstalling the app from your Atlassian site stops all access immediately. Questions: buahrasa01@gmail.com.
